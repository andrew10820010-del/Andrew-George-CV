Breadfast

Production Rate & Line Balance Dashboard Sandwiches & Salads

~83%avg Morning efficiency (Sandwiches)
~81%avg Between efficiency (Sandwiches)
~3.2 hwasted / Morning shift
~2.6 hwasted / Between shift
  • Two factories / lines monitored: Sandwiches (10 SKUs) and Salads (7 SKUs) across Morning and Between shifts, with daily plan quantities, actual output, PMH and estimated hours.
  • Efficiency & waste visibility: real working hours vs available capacity. Morning Sandwiches ~4.8 h productive vs ~3.2 h wasted; Between ~5.4 h productive vs ~2.6 h wasted. Salads efficiencies lower (~68–74%), highlighting larger opportunity.
  • Line balancing insights: process-level cycle times (e.g. Turkey & Cheese, Smoked Salmon Bagel) expose bottlenecks such as “Adding Turkey & Rumy” (~3:50). Workers can be reallocated from high-PMH stations to constrained ones to raise overall rate without extra headcount.
  • Actionable KPIs: PMH by product, plan vs actual, shift totals, monthly averages and rest-of-capacity so supervisors see where to focus standard work and reduce waiting / changeover loss.
  • Impact path: raise effective production rate by cutting wasted hours and balancing the line; same workforce, higher daily output and more stable flow.
